The studio

Cyclamen Studio is a one-person studio making calm, good-looking games for phones: towns you can read at a glance, economies that reward a little planning, and sessions you can put down mid-thought and pick up a week later.

Behind the sunshine sits serious machinery. Flat Peaks runs on a deterministic simulation with thousands of automated tests, so the town you built last month still works after every update.

Every email is read by the person who made the game.
