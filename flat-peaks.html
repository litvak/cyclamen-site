Flat Peaks

Build the postcard. Live the vacation.

Flat Peaks is a cozy city-builder where you bring a vintage travel poster to life, one hillside village at a time.

Every village is a poster you can live in. Flat Peaks starts on The Island, a small hill on the Amalfi coast with an old lighthouse and a harbour that has fallen into ruin. You draw the first road up the slope, build the first houses, and watch residents walk to work through the lanes.

Discover the place through what it makes

Farms grow herbs, tomatoes and citrus. Terraces and pergolas carry olives, lemons and vines. Each production chain is a small piece of local culture: milk becomes cheese, olives become oil, lemons become limoncello, and bread is a village craft too. You unlock new crafts, shops and recipes as the village grows.

Keep your village happy

Residents come when the village is joyful: well fed, well supplied, and nice to look at. Bancarelle market stalls, cafés, trattorias and gardens all add to the life of the town.

Restore the island

Relight the lighthouse, repair the old pier, and reopen the harbour to the sea. The lighthouse is in the game now; the pier, fishing and harbour trade are still to come.

Then travel

The Island is free to play and is your home port. New destinations are coming as chapters, each a new poster town with its own landscape, crafts and flavours. Premium chapters are bought once each and kept. Each one connects back to your island by sea, opening trade between the places you’ve built.

What’s inside

  • A living travel poster. Hand-illustrated scenes in a warm 1950s–70s Mediterranean print style, with parallax hills.
  • Cozy, stress-free building. Play at your own pace. The game never punishes you.
  • Culture as progression. Unlock real regional crafts and recipes, not factory tiers.
  • A village that moves. Residents commute, carts deliver goods, and fields come to life as you work them.
  • Story landmarks. Relight the lighthouse now. The harbour repairs and trade pier are still to come.
  • A network of destinations. Start free on The Island, then unlock new poster towns linked by trade.
  • Made for phones. Portrait, touch-first, and playable one-handed.

What you won’t

  • Watch ads, or wait on timers built to sell you a shortcut.
  • Make an account or sign in.
  • Need a connection. Your town lives on your device and is never uploaded. The one exception is an optional crash report, which you can switch off — see Privacy.
