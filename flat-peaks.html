Flat Peaks

A cozy coastal city-builder

Grow olives, lemons and wheat on terraced hills. Turn them into flour, cheese and pastries. Lay roads, place houses, and watch a small Mediterranean town take shape at whatever pace suits you.

There is no fail state and nothing is on a timer. Flat Peaks is a builder you can put down mid-thought and pick up a week later, drawn in the flat colours and soft grain of a 1960s travel poster.

What you do

  • Farm the hills. Olive groves, lemon orchards and wheat fields, each with their own rhythm.
  • Run the production chains. Wheat becomes flour becomes bread; milk becomes cheese; the chains deepen as the town grows.
  • Plan the roads. Every building needs connecting, and road upkeep comes out of your budget.
  • Balance the books. A period budget tracks income against upkeep, so growth has to pay for itself.

No strings

  • No advertisements.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No account, no sign-in.
  • Plays entirely offline. Your town is saved on your device.
