Flat Peaks

A cozy coastal city-builder

A small harbour town, a hillside of terraces, and all the time in the world.

Flat Peaks is a city-builder in the flat colours and soft grain of a 1960s travel poster. Plant the slopes, connect the houses, and turn the harvest into the bread and cheese your town lives on. There is no fail state and nothing on a timer, so it suits ten minutes as well as an afternoon.

What you’ll do

  • Farm the hills. Olive groves, lemon orchards and wheat fields, each with its own rhythm.
  • Build production chains. Wheat to flour to bread, milk to cheese, and longer chains as the town grows.
  • Lay the roads. Every building needs a connection, and every stretch of road has upkeep.
  • Keep the books. Income against upkeep, period by period, so growth has to pay for itself.

What you won’t

  • Watch ads or buy anything.
  • Make an account or sign in.
  • Need a connection. Your town lives on your device and is never uploaded. The one exception is an optional crash report, which you can switch off — see Privacy.
