Flat Peaks

Privacy Policy

Last revised 17 September 2026.

Flat Peaks, published by Cyclamen Studio, does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account to create and no server to talk to.

What we collect

Nothing. Flat Peaks contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no crash-reporting service. The app does not send information about you or your device anywhere.

What the game stores

Your saved towns, settings and progress are written to local storage on your own device. They are never uploaded. They are not backed up to us, because there is no "us" to back them up to — we have no servers.

Uninstalling Flat Peaks deletes this data permanently. You can also clear it from within the game at any time by starting a new game. Because the data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Permissions

Flat Peaks requests a single Android permission, INTERNET, which is a technical requirement of the framework the game is built on. The game plays fully offline and makes no network requests of its own.

It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or any other sensitive permission.

Children

Flat Peaks is suitable for all ages and contains no user-to-user communication, no user-generated content, and no purchases. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect information from children, and is consistent with COPPA and comparable children's privacy rules.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Flat Peaks ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the revision date above will change. Any such change would also be reflected in the app's Data Safety entry on Google Play.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@cyclamen-games.com.
