Flat Peaks

Support

Stuck, or something broken? Most answers are below. For anything else, email support@cyclamen-games.com and the person who made the game will reply.

If you are reporting a problem, it helps enormously to include your device model, your Android version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Where are my saved games?

On your device, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere, so they do not follow you to a second device and cannot be restored by us.

How do I start over?

Start a new game from the main menu. This replaces your current town, and the old one cannot be recovered afterwards.

I uninstalled the game. Can I get my town back?

No. Uninstalling removes the local save permanently. There is no cloud copy, because the game never sends your data off the device.

What do I need to run it?

Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer. Flat Peaks renders with WebGL, so it wants a device with working hardware graphics acceleration — most phones from the last several years qualify comfortably.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every part of the game — building, saving, progressing — works with no connection at all. The only thing that ever reaches a server is the optional Crash reports setting described below, and it changes nothing about how the game plays if you turn it off.

Are there ads or purchases?

Neither. There is nothing to buy and nothing to watch.

Does the game send anything over the network?

Only if you leave the optional Crash reports setting on (in the Settings menu). Turn it off at any time and nothing further is sent. See the Privacy Policy for what a report can contain.

How do I join the test track?

Flat Peaks is in closed testing. Email the address above and you will be sent an opt-in link.
